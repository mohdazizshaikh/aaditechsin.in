Frequently asked questions

Do you supply specific brands?

We can evaluate compatible brands and models based on availability, budget and verified partner capabilities; no brand endorsement is assumed.

Can existing equipment be reused?

Yes, where compatible and safe. We assess condition and system requirements before recommending reuse.

Do you guarantee sound coverage?

Coverage and intelligibility depend on room acoustics, equipment and installation; any performance commitment must be defined in an agreed scope.
