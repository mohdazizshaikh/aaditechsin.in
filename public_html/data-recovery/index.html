Frequently asked questions

Is successful recovery guaranteed?

No. Outcome depends on storage condition, prior writes, firmware, controller, encryption and physical damage.

Should I run CHKDSK or format the drive?

Avoid formatting, reinstalling or repeatedly running repair tools on a failing device before professional advice.

Can you arrange a specialist partner?

We coordinate suitable recovery partners depending on device, problem, location and available capacity. Costs and custody arrangements are confirmed before handover.
