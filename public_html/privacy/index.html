Inquiry privacy notice

This is an implementation draft. Aaditechs must verify the actual data-processing arrangements, retention period and business contact details before accepting live inquiries.

What the form asks for

Only after enabled and explicitly submitted: your name, email, optional phone, requested service, project details, and contact consent. Do not send passwords, confidential files, access credentials or sensitive records.

How information would be used

Aaditechs would use this information to assess and respond to your request, prepare scopes or quotations, and coordinate appropriate service delivery. Partner access is restricted; customer contact details are not displayed in the partner portal by default. Any external sharing beyond that will require appropriate notice and arrangements.

Storage and security

When the production lead system is enabled, records are intended to be stored on the configured server database and available only to authenticated staff. No lead data is stored by the static demonstration mode. Website access should use HTTPS. Admins must set retention and deletion policies.

Optional first-party analytics (planned, disabled in source)

If separately enabled after review, the site asks permission before sending optional page and service events. It stores a local preference and a random tab-session identifier after permission; the server stores a day-scoped keyed hash, page/service identifiers, broad source category, event ID and an automation signal. It does not collect form text, full referrer URLs, contact details or raw IP addresses in analytics records. Ordinary hosting access logs are separate. These counts are approximate, not verified people, calls or messages. You can turn analytics off on public service pages. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals prevent collection. Proposed event retention is 30 days and must be reviewed and configured before activation; turning off stops future collection, not previously recorded events.

Contact / questions

Email info@aaditechs.in to ask about an inquiry or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements and verified ownership.

Do not enable real customer data collection until this notice has been reviewed and adapted to the actual hosting and partner arrangements.
