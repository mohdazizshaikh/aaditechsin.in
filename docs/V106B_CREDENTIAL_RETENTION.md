## Communications retention

communications.retention_enabled independently OFF. Before any real cleanup, obtain owner-approved retention policy, verify backup and isolated restore, stop workers and pause growth. Dry-run rolls back and prints counts, cutoffs and plan SHA256, not IDs or mailbox. Explicit --apply PLAN_SHA256 recomputes locked candidates and cutoff; changed digest or unpaused growth refuses. Deletes only planned eligible records in a transaction, checks affected counts and records sanitized audit. The 90-day boundary matches existing import eligibility, so purged older days cannot be reimported through normal import.
